How a verdict is reached

Each product is checked daily on Amazon.in. A listing needs at least 7 days of price history before PriceSpy will call it. If today's price sits within 3% of the 7-day average, it's marked REAL. If a sudden "discount" appears right after a price spike, it's marked FAKE. Everything else stays TRACKING until there's enough evidence.
