How a verdict is reached

For every result, PriceSpy compares the listed price against the original MRP shown on the same page. A claimed discount under 15% is treated as modest — too small to judge. Between 15–40% it's marked Likely Real. Above 40%, the discount gets flagged as Verify Before Buying — large claimed discounts are the most commonly inflated kind, where the "original price" was never really the selling price.
